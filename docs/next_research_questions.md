# Future research agenda (not completed results)

1. Does a covariance-aware TAR predict nonlinear relational tasks under kernel or class-conditional covariance contrasts, without leakage or excessive parameters?
2. At matched scalar embedding budget and FLOPs, do learned overlapping stems preserve task signal better than non-overlapping ones?
3. How does finite-precision quantization change effective injectivity of ill-conditioned full-rank embeddings?
4. Are geometry-based signals measured on well-characterized natural patch distributions correlated with held-out error for pretrained/fine-tuned ViTs?
5. Could a common learned encoder be near-sufficient for several tasks but catastrophically insufficient for an unforeseen task, and how should one quantify task portfolios?
6. Would learned class-conditional covariance estimators outperform mean TAR on XOR while remaining useful out of distribution?
