# Final revision literature update - 15 November 2026

The final paper supersedes the preliminary implementation descriptions below. Adaptive H-score and profile-evidence LogME are now main baselines. Ibrahim et al. is cited as the published ECML PKDD 2022 proceedings chapter (online 2023), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-26387-3_42.

Agostinelli et al., How Stable Are Transferability Metrics Evaluations? (ECCV 2022), already establish evaluation-design sensitivity: https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136940296.pdf. Singh, Hess and Vanschoren, How NOT to benchmark your SITE metric (ICLR 2026), already identify static candidate hierarchies and strong dataset-agnostic heuristics: https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/f0ebc318e2df08360b2df559e81602e5-Abstract-Conference.html. Their findings are prior art, not our novelty claims.

Our restricted extension is a shared linear raw-pixel patch bank with matched scalar dimension, within-p projection controls, full-test content-clean splits and an external SVHN fixed-policy evaluation. It remains much smaller than broad transferability benchmarks. LEEP and NCE are discussed with their required source classifier/label assumptions; those outputs are unavailable for DCT/PCA/random patch maps. The current bibliography also cites the SVHN source and Ledoit-Wolf covariance estimator.

## Scope of the defensible contribution

The mathematical tools are established: permutation/rank arguments, Gaussian discrimination, a Beta ratio of isotropic quadratic forms, cross-validated contrasts and shrinkage covariance. The refined manuscript can offer a reproducible **controlled audit** of these diagnostics for frozen patch operators under fixed output dimension, including real images, actual pretrained matrices, label-budget/conditioning sensitivity and optimization controls. Its value must come from the measured comparisons and practical limitations, not renaming a known score.

The equal-output-budget bank deliberately makes global rank uninformative, while within-p comparisons also match dense projection dimensions. These are complementary controls; neither matches whole-Transformer compute. All stronger conclusions about general pretrained ViT performance, unseen domains or a universally better selector remain unsupported unless separately established.

## Source verification and method fidelity

- The CIFAR publisher specifies official train/test partitions and archive checksums: [dataset page](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html). The mirrored archives are accepted only after those exact checksums pass.
- Our LogME baseline uses centered, scalar-RMS-normalized features, uncentered one-hot targets, eleven fixed-point iterations with the published ICML-style tolerance, and a final exact evidence evaluation at the returned hyperparameters. This is a documented implementation variant, not bit-for-bit replication of every released implementation revision. Tests compare its objective with direct integrated Gaussian likelihood.
- The primary fixed-0.1 shrunk H-score is rank-equivalent to adding the constant between-class shrink factor at that fixed alpha. It does not reproduce the adaptive estimator. The exploratory adaptive score includes the `(1-alpha)` factor and estimates alpha from diagnostic features without using test labels.
- The model-geometry audit evaluates SVD in float64 on stored float32 weights and reports a dtype-aware rank convention separately. Full column rank in exact arithmetic does not imply stable inversion after quantization/noise.
