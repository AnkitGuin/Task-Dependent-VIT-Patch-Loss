# Response to methodological peer review

Manuscript: **Selecting Frozen Linear Patch Operators: An Empirical Audit under Matched Feature Budgets**. Author: **Ankit Guin**. Revision date: 15 November 2026.

The review correctly identified the limited empirical scope, baseline-convergence problems and lack of a convincing practical fixed-policy comparison. The revision treats the previous two-CIFAR experiment and its exploratory extensions as preliminary. Main conclusions now come from the separately specified final v2 cohort. The earlier manuscript is preserved rather than retrospectively relabeled confirmatory. Acceptance and quartile suitability are not guaranteed by these changes.

| Review concern | Revision and supporting material |
|---|---|
| Weak novelty differentiation | Retitled and reorganized around frozen shared linear raw-pixel patch selection; no new Gaussian theorem, shrinkage method or universal selector is claimed. Related work now includes controlled evaluation instability and 2026 evidence about static candidate hierarchies and fixed heuristics. Main contribution is the explicitly controlled empirical decision/cost audit. |
| Only related CIFAR datasets | Added cropped SVHN with native RGB resolution, its entire 26,032-image official test set, five fresh seeds and both feature budgets. SVHN is one external domain; repeated seeds are not counted as independent problems. |
| Tiny gaps and design-dependent winner | Added domain/budget absolute accuracies and regret, within-patch-size comparisons, fractional candidate frequencies, paired seed-only and joint image/seed intervals, and comparisons against fixed DCT. No universal or equivalence claim follows from a small difference or an interval containing zero. |
| Uncompetitive LogME/H-score/regularization | Profile the same Gaussian evidence objective with broad search, converged bounded refinements and an analytic null limit; abort on refinement failure. Adaptive Ledoit--Wolf H-score is a main baseline. All final heads use one validation-only thirteen-value ridge grid from 10^-2 through 10^10; the narrower partial revision is excluded and its amendment documented. |
| Selection may add no value over a fixed rule | Lock p04_dct at both budgets using already observed historical CIFAR evidence, before any SVHN outcomes. Report external SVHN gains separately. Revised CIFAR results are descriptive, not independent policy validation. |
| Small test subsets and false independent replication | Use every official test image: 10,000 per CIFAR dataset and 26,032 for SVHN. The paired bootstrap shares image weights across all seeds, budgets and selectors, then resamples training-seed blocks. Domains receive equal aggregate weight, with no claim of population-domain inference. |
| Exact-content leakage | Deduplicate training before every final operator/head fit. CIFAR-100 removes ten training rows matching test content, eighteen rows in conflicting-label groups and five further repetitions; SVHN removes two conflicting-label training rows. Full official test sets remain unchanged. Historical exclusion-only deltas are explicitly tabulated and distinguished from a refit correction. |
| Insufficient practical resource evidence | Fresh-process benchmark covers every selector, all three domains and both budgets, repeated three times at two threads. Cold time includes actual operator-bank fitting; warm time includes every method's extraction/statistics/scoring. Peak and incremental RSS include cold construction. Warm-ups overlapping experiments are excluded. Selected-head fitting is recorded separately. |
| Reproducibility incomplete | Supply locked dependencies, configs, exact official IDs/content hashes, source fingerprints, operators/heads, validation curves, pretest choices, full predictions, independent NumPy/direct-solve replay and table generation. Source and evidence ZIPs have per-file SHA256 manifests and complete archive verification. These are local release artifacts; public upload and a real persistent URL remain necessary before submission. |
| Multiple competing narratives | Move the pretrained-rank, null-calibration and XOR training-budget evidence to appendices. Expand the former small combined panels into separate readable figures. Main results now answer the practical selection question. |

Additional corrections: the published Ibrahim et al. ECML PKDD 2022 chapter (online publication 2023) replaces its arXiv citation and includes DOI `10.1007/978-3-031-26387-3_42`. The focused bibliography now includes SVHN, Ledoit--Wolf shrinkage, evaluation stability, LEEP/NCE scope and current benchmark criticism. The paper distinguishes 1,000 diagnostic labels, 2,000 head-training labels and 500 validation labels, reports predetermined covariance shrinkage sensitivity, inspects H-score by operator family and domain, and retains its empirical limitations.
